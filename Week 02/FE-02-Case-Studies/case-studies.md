# Portfolio Case Studies

## Project 1 – InterviewAce AI

### Problem

Many students prepare for technical interviews by watching videos or reading questions, but they don't receive personalised practice or feedback. I wanted to create a platform that simulates interviews and provides meaningful AI-generated evaluation.

### What I Did

I built InterviewAce AI using Flask, Python, SQLite, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I integrated an AI model to generate role-specific interview questions, evaluate user responses, and provide constructive feedback. I also implemented authentication, response storage, and a clean interface so users could track their interview practice over time.

### Outcome

InterviewAce AI became a complete interview preparation platform that demonstrates my ability to integrate AI into a full-stack application, manage backend logic, and deploy a working web application.

## Project 2 – CampusSync

### Problem

College announcements, events, and notices are often scattered across multiple communication channels, making it difficult for students to stay informed.

### What I Did

I developed CampusSync as a centralized platform where students, faculty, and administrators can manage announcements and events. I implemented authentication, role-based access control, faculty approval workflows, and database integration to ensure secure content management.

### Outcome

The project improved my understanding of authentication, user roles, and full-stack application architecture while solving a practical communication problem.

## Project 3 – Spendora

### Problem

Many people struggle to track their daily expenses and understand their spending habits.

### What I Did

I contributed to developing Spendora, an expense tracking application with a clean user interface and responsive design. I focused on building reusable frontend components, improving user experience, and integrating expense management features.

### Outcome

The project strengthened my frontend development skills and reinforced the importance of designing interfaces that are both functional and intuitive.
