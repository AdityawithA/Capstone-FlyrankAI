# Voice Card

## My Writing Voice

**Clear, practical, honest, confident, approachable, concise.**

## Writing Principles

- Explain technical work in simple language.
- Focus on real decisions instead of buzzwords.
- Show evidence through projects rather than making broad claims.
- Be direct and avoid unnecessary filler.
- Write like I speak in a professional technical discussion.
- Keep every sentence purposeful and authentic.

## Audience

Software engineering recruiters and hiring managers looking for an intern or junior full-stack developer.

## Goal

Help recruiters quickly understand how I think, build software, and solve real-world problems through practical projects.
