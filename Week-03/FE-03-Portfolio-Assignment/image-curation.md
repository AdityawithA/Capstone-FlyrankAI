## Screenshot Standards
- Use the real deployed application.
- Capture clean desktop/mobile views where useful.
- Crop consistently and keep important UI legible.
- Remove browser clutter where possible.
- Never fabricate metrics or results.

## Rejection Note
I rejected a generic AI-generated technology hero because it looked impressive but communicated no real proof about my work. It also competed with the project screenshots.

I also rejected a glossy glassmorphism AI illustration because it made the portfolio feel like a generic AI template. Real screenshots of InterviewAce AI and the other projects provide stronger and more credible proof.
