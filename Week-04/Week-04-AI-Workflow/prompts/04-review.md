Review the draft as a strict fact-checking editor.

Check:
1. Are all factual claims supported by the sources?
2. Are any claims exaggerated?
3. Are any important details missing?
4. Are sources correctly associated with claims?
5. Is anything presented as fact when it is actually interpretation?
6. Are there duplicated points?
7. Is the writing concise and professional?
8. Can a reader understand the report without reading the original sources?

For every problem you find:
- identify the problem
- explain why it is a problem
- provide a corrected version

Do not introduce new facts that are not present in the sources.

After the review, provide a final corrected version of the brief.
