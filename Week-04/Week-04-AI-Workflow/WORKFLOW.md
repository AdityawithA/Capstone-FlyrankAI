# Workflow Walkthrough

## Goal
Turn real AI/web-development research sources into a concise weekly industry brief.

## Flow

REAL SOURCES
↓
1. GATHER — extract supported facts and developments
↓
2. SYNTHESIZE — group findings and identify importance
↓
3. DRAFT — create the weekly brief
↓
4. REVIEW + FORMAT — fact-check and polish
↓
FINAL WEEKLY BRIEF

## Step 1 — Gather
Input: real source material.
Output: structured research notes.

## Step 2 — Synthesize
Input: research notes and sources.
Output: grouped insights with source support.

## Step 3 — Draft
Input: synthesized research.
Output: professional weekly brief.

## Step 4 — Review + Format
Input: draft and sources.
Output: corrected final brief.

## Five Runs
The assignment requires five real inputs. Complete `runs/run-01` through `runs/run-05` with actual runs and outputs.

## Time Accounting
Measure manual work and workflow time separately. Include initial setup time rather than hiding it.

## Failure Points
- Source quality affects output quality.
- Missing context can lead to weak conclusions.
- Conflicting sources require human investigation.
- Interpretation can be presented too confidently.
- Poor source selection limits the workflow.

## Human Review
A human must verify important facts, dates, numbers, source credibility, conflicts, conclusions, and unsupported claims before publication.
