# Final Walkthrough

## Assignment
Build a no-code AI research/writing pipeline with three or more distinct steps, run it on five real inputs, measure time saved, and document failure points and required human review.

## Chosen Pipeline
Weekly AI & Web Development Industry Brief.

## Tool
NotebookLM.

## Steps
1. Gather
2. Synthesize
3. Draft
4. Review + Format

## Evidence
Add the five real runs and their outputs from the `runs/` folders.

## Time Saved
Record:
- Manual time
- Workflow execution time
- Initial setup time
- Total estimated time saved

## Failure Points
Record issues observed during the five real runs.

## Human Review
Verify facts, dates, numbers, sources, conflicting claims, interpretation, and final relevance before publication.

## Submission Note
Do not claim the five runs or time savings are complete until the actual workflow has been run and measured.
