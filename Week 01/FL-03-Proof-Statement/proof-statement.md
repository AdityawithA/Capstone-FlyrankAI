# FL-03 Proof Statement

## Primary Claim

I build production-ready full-stack web applications that solve real-world problems.

## Target Audience

Software engineering recruiters and hiring managers seeking intern or junior full-stack developers.

## Desired Action

Invite me for a technical interview.

## Proof Statement

I build production-ready full-stack web applications that solve real-world problems using modern technologies such as React, Node.js, Express.js, SQL, and AI-powered tools. This portfolio is designed for software engineering recruiters and hiring managers who are looking for an intern or junior full-stack developer capable of turning ideas into functional, well-structured applications. Through real projects, clean code, live deployments, and documented development practices, I aim to demonstrate my ability to contribute to professional software teams and encourage recruiters to invite me for an interview.

## Why This Portfolio Exists

A resume and LinkedIn profile list my skills and projects, but they cannot fully demonstrate how I design, build, and deploy complete applications. This portfolio provides tangible proof of my technical ability and problem-solving approach.
