# FL-03 AI Interview

## Objective

Refine my portfolio proof statement by using AI as a thinking partner rather than an author.

---

## Initial Proof Statement

I build production-ready full-stack web applications that solve real-world problems using modern technologies such as React, Node.js, Express.js, SQL, and AI-powered tools. This portfolio is designed for software engineering recruiters and hiring managers who are looking for an intern or junior full-stack developer capable of turning ideas into functional, well-structured applications. Through real projects, clean code, live deployments, and documented development practices, I aim to demonstrate my ability to contribute to professional software teams and encourage recruiters to invite me for an interview.

---

## AI Interview

### AI Question 1

**AI:** What evidence proves that you build production-ready applications rather than just academic projects?

**My Answer:** My portfolio includes deployed full-stack applications such as InterviewAce AI, CampusSync, Spendora, and my personal portfolio website. These projects include authentication, databases, REST APIs, deployment, and version control.

---

### AI Question 2

**AI:** Why should a recruiter believe your claim instead of similar claims made by other candidates?

**My Answer:** My projects are publicly available with live demos and GitHub repositories. Recruiters can review my code, deployment, and development process rather than relying only on my resume.

---

### AI Question 3

**AI:** Who is the specific audience for this portfolio?

**My Answer:** Software engineering recruiters and hiring managers who are hiring interns or junior full-stack developers.

---

### AI Question 4

**AI:** What single action do you want visitors to take after viewing your portfolio?

**My Answer:** I want recruiters to contact me for a technical interview.

---

## Reflection

The AI helped me make my claim more specific by asking for evidence, identifying my target audience, and clarifying the primary action. I updated my proof statement to focus on measurable work rather than broad descriptions.
