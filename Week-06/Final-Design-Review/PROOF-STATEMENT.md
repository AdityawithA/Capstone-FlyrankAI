# Portfolio Proof Statement

## What I do

I am a Computer Science and Design student focused on software development, full-stack web applications, and practical AI/ML projects.

## What I am building toward

My current direction is AI engineering, supported by a strong full-stack development foundation.

## What this portfolio should prove

The portfolio should show that I can:

- build usable software rather than only prototypes,
- work across frontend and backend development,
- integrate AI into practical applications,
- work with APIs and databases,
- deploy projects,
- explain the technical decisions behind my work,
- and turn ideas into working products.

## What a reviewer should be able to find

A visitor should be able to quickly find:

- my role and technical direction,
- strong projects such as InterviewAce AI and CampusSync,
- live demos and/or repositories,
- my GitHub and LinkedIn,
- and a clear way to contact me.

## Review standard

The portfolio should communicate its value without requiring me to explain it personally.

The two questions used for the review are:

1. **In ten seconds, what do I do?**
2. **Would you believe I'm good at it? Why or why not?**
