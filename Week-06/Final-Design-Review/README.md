## Portfolio job

The portfolio should make three things clear quickly:

1. Who Aditya is.
2. What he builds and what technical direction he is pursuing.
3. Where the evidence is — projects, live work, repositories, and contact links.

## Prepared review outcome

The main strength is that the portfolio clearly presents a software-development identity and contains concrete project work.

The main improvement area is **message clarity and proof hierarchy**. A visitor should not have to scan multiple sections to understand the primary role, strongest projects, and next action.

## Must-fixes identified

### 1. Make the primary positioning immediately obvious
The hero should communicate the core identity in one short sentence rather than making the visitor infer it from several labels.

**Fix:** Lead with a direct positioning statement around software/full-stack development and AI engineering.

### 2. Make project proof easier to scan
Projects are the strongest evidence, so project cards should make the project name, purpose, stack, and live/repository proof obvious.

**Fix:** Keep the strongest projects first and make the action links visually clear.

### 3. Strengthen the primary contact action
The visitor should have an obvious next step after understanding the work.

**Fix:** Keep the contact/LinkedIn/GitHub actions visible and consistent.

### 4. Keep mobile readability as a first-class requirement
The portfolio is expected to work on phones, not only on desktop.

**Fix:** Check navigation, spacing, typography, cards, buttons, and project links at narrow widths.
