# Must-Fix Log

**Portfolio:** https://aditya-kumar-flyrankai.netlify.app/

This log records the fixes recommended by the design review.

## 01 — Clarify positioning

**Problem:** The visitor should not have to infer the primary role.

**Fix:** Make the hero explicitly communicate full-stack development + practical AI.

**Expected result:** A recruiter can identify the candidate's direction within a few seconds.

---

## 02 — Prioritize project proof

**Problem:** Every project can appear equally important.

**Fix:** Put InterviewAce AI and CampusSync near the top, followed by the other strongest work.

**Expected result:** The first projects provide the majority of credibility.

---

## 03 — Strengthen action links

**Problem:** A portfolio needs a clear continuation path.

**Fix:** Keep GitHub, LinkedIn, project demos, and contact actions obvious and consistently labelled.

**Expected result:** Visitors can immediately inspect work or contact the candidate.

---

## 04 — Mobile verification

**Problem:** Desktop layouts can hide mobile-specific usability problems.

**Fix:** Verify navigation, typography, cards, spacing, buttons and links on a real phone.

**Expected result:** The portfolio remains usable at narrow widths.
